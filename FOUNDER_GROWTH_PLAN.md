# TEYO founder growth plan

## Reality check

I can help build, improve, and automate the TEYO experience, but I cannot replace the hard parts of building a company: outreach, partnerships, trust, reputation, and execution.

If the goal is to become a major marketplace, the realistic path is not to become "Amazon" overnight. The realistic path is to become the best operating layer for a specific niche and then expand.

## What I can do for you

- improve the website experience and conversion flow
- add guided assistants for Guard and consultations
- add onboarding, checklists, and safer flows
- improve the partner and company experience
- add analytics hooks, lead capture, and automation-ready structure
- help you organize the product into a stronger business engine

## What you must do

- reach out to real companies and stores directly
- get a small number of pilot partners live
- gather real inventory and real stock data
- build trust and reputation with early users
- follow up repeatedly until the product proves value

## The next 30-day focus

### 1. Pick one niche first
Choose one vertical instead of trying to be everything.

Good starting niches:
- luxury retail and premium stores
- family offices and wealth-adjacent services
- high-end apparel or accessories
- boutique brands with limited inventory
- local premium retail groups

### 2. Get 5-10 real pilot partners
You need a small number of real partners before the product feels real.

Target:
- 3 stores
- 3 brands
- 2-4 companies or operators

### 3. Put real inventory into the system
Do not rely on fake or placeholder data.

Start with:
- 10-20 live products
- real stock status
- real sizes
- real restock notes

### 4. Use the site as a sales tool
The website should not just look good. It should help a partner understand:
- what TEYO is
- why it helps them
- what the next step is

### 5. Build a simple follow-up cadence
Every lead should get:
- a welcome message
- a simple walkthrough
- a specific next step
- a deadline

## The growth engine

The strongest path is:
1. attract a few real companies
2. give them a useful dashboard
3. make their inventory visible
4. make the experience feel premium and trustworthy
5. let the product spread by word of mouth

## What to say to prospects

Use this simple message:

"We help premium companies and stores make their inventory, stock availability, and operating visibility more useful and more trustworthy through a protected, premium digital experience. We are onboarding a small group of early partners and would love to show you how it could fit your business."

## What not to do

- do not try to become everything at once
- do not chase broad consumer traffic first
- do not rely only on branding or content
- do not wait for the site to magically attract users

## Bottom line

TEYO has the bones of a real business. It is not yet a giant marketplace. The fastest path is to win a narrow niche, get real partners, and make the product valuable in practice.
